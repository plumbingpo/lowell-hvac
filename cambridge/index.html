Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in Cambridge

Many mid-season breakdowns start with skipped maintenance, and the habits that prevent them are easy. Change your filter every one to three months, keep plants and shrubs at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and rinse the condenser coil every spring. Then book a professional tune-up twice a year, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

Age matters too. Most central air conditioners last 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15 with regular service. If your system is past that window and starting to struggle, such as rising energy bills, rooms that never feel comfortable, or repairs stacking up season after season, a planned replacement is almost always cheaper and far less stressful than an emergency one in the middle of a cold snap.

The earlier you report a comfort problem, the cheaper it usually is. A capacitor that's starting to fail is an easy, low-cost part to replace, yet that same capacitor, ignored for a season, can take out the compressor and force a full system replacement.
